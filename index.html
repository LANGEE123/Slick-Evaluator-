Spreadsheet Format & University Rounding Policy

The uploaded Excel file should contain three columns: BITS ID (e.g. 2024A7PS0101P), Course, and Total Marks.

Total Marks must be on a 0–100 scale. Fractional marks are automatically rounded to the nearest integer as per academic policy (e.g. 80.2 → 81).

Students absent from the exam who are to receive an NC grade should not be included in this file.
